My Aya,

On 21 August 2025 my life quietly changed direction, and I didn't even know it yet. I only knew that every road I took somehow started leading back to you.

They say your name means a sign. I believe it with my whole heart, because you are the clearest sign I have ever been given: that God was kind to me, that love is real, and that it has your face.

I love you in ways I still can't find the words for. I love your laugh, the real one, the one that makes me forget whatever was worrying me. I love the way you care for the people you love with everything you have. I love that when the whole world is loud, your voice is the only one I'm listening for.

And this year, I don't want to just tell you that I love you. I want to build it. A home with both our names on the door. Mornings where the first thing I see is you, and nights where I never have to say goodbye to you again. And one day, a family of our own, with your heart and your smile in every one of them.

Until then, and long after, here is my promise. I will choose you every single day. On the easy days and on the hard ones. When we are young and when we are grey. I will hold your hand in every room we ever live in, and I will never stop being grateful that it's yours I get to hold.

You are my home before we even have one. You are my answered prayer. You are my sign, my love, my whole life.

Happy Valentine's Day, my love. Here's to our second Valentine's, and to every one after.

Yours, today and every day after,Your husband
